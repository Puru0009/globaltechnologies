Do you give a warranty?

It depends on the product or repair. Some parts and products carry our warranty, some carry the manufacturer's warranty, and some, such as old or used parts, carry none. We tell you which applies before you pay.

Your files are your business. Tell us if your device holds sensitive data and we will talk through the safest way to handle the repair. If you can, back up important files before you bring it in.
